隐私政策

时间花园 Time Garden ·（iOS App）· 生效日期：2026-07-26

一句话版本：我们不收集任何数据。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。时间花园没有账号系统、没有服务器、没有分析或统计 SDK、没有广告、没有任何第三方跟踪代码。App 中不存在把你的数据发送给开发者或任何第三方的代码。App 隐私标签为「未收集数据（Data Not Collected）」。

你的数据存在哪里

你的事件与植物数据只保存在这台设备上。App 完全离线运行，不申请相机、相册、定位等任何敏感权限；分享卡与植物标本导出走 iOS 系统分享面板，仅在你主动操作时发生。

App 内购买

购买与恢复购买由 Apple 的 App Store（StoreKit）直接处理。我们不经手、也无法看到你的支付信息；这是本 App 唯一的网络活动，且由系统发起。

政策变更

若未来版本引入新的数据实践（例如可选的 iCloud 私有库同步——即便引入，数据也只进入你自己的 iCloud 账户，开发者无法访问），我们会先更新本政策并在更新说明中告知。

联系我们

关于隐私的任何疑问，请通过 App Store 产品页的「App 支持」链接与我们联系。

Privacy Policy

Time Garden (iOS app) · Effective date: 2026-07-26

The short version: we collect no data. None.

What we collect

Nothing. Time Garden has no accounts, no servers, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no ads, and no third-party code that could transmit your data. There is no code path in the app that sends anything to the developer or to any third party. Its App Privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

Where your data lives

Your events and plants are stored only on this device. The app runs fully offline and requests no sensitive permissions (no camera, no photo library, no location); share cards and herbarium exports use the iOS share sheet and happen only when you initiate them.

In-app purchases

Purchases and restores are handled directly by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We never see or touch your payment information. This is the app's only network activity, and it is initiated by the system.

Changes

If a future version ever introduces a new data practice (for example, optional iCloud private-database sync — which would put data only in your own iCloud account, inaccessible to the developer), we will update this policy first and say so in the release notes.

Contact

For any privacy question, reach us via the “App Support” link on the App Store product page.
